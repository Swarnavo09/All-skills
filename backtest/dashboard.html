NIFTY synthetic futures: trend strategy backtest

Read this first: the NSE bhavcopy source only has daily candles, with no 30-minute bars and no option premiums. So every rule here runs on daily candles, and the synthetic future (buy ATM CE + sell ATM PE) is modelled as NIFTY spot points × quantity, minus the cost of carry. A daily candle doesn't show whether its high or its low came first, so the backtest assumes the worse order by default (switch it under Intrabar order). Treat these results as directional evidence, not a verdict on the 30-minute version.
